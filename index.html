Language & voice

Voice input and read-aloud use on-device speech when your browser supports it. In most browsers, online speech sends your audio to the browser maker, so it stays off unless you tick the box.

Privacy controls

Emails, phone numbers, Aadhaar, PAN, card numbers, IPs and API keys are swapped for placeholders like [EMAIL_1]. The model never sees the real value; the originals are put back in your view only.

What this page does and doesn't do with your data
  • No server of mine sits in the middle. Requests go from your browser directly to the provider you pick.
  • No analytics, cookies, fonts or scripts from other sites. The browser enforces this with a Content-Security-Policy.
  • Chats are kept in memory only. Closing the tab erases them. Export is manual.
  • Your key stays in memory unless you choose otherwise; the encrypted option uses AES-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2, 310,000 rounds).
  • The provider you choose still receives what you send, under its own privacy policy. For zero third-party exposure, use Ollama or LM Studio on your own computer.
